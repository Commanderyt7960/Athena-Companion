ATHENA V13.2 - ANDROID STARTUP / WAKE CRASH FIX

Based on the V13 source and the reported symptom: the app opens, then immediately closes.

Changes:
- Background microphone service startup is delayed until the Activity is fully resumed.
- MainActivity catches SecurityException/IllegalStateException/other startup failures.
- AthenaWakeService now catches foreground-service startup failures instead of allowing an uncaught exception to kill the app process.
- SpeechRecognizer creation is guarded.
- Existing UI, local model, PC pairing and accessibility features are otherwise unchanged.

The JNI strip message for libllm_inference_engine_jni.so is packaging-only and is not treated as a build failure.
