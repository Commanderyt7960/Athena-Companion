ATHENA V14 - ANDROID STARTUP HARDENING
======================================

This rebuild keeps V13.2 functionality and adds a stricter permission/startup sequence.

- The app UI is built and the local model load is started before permission prompting.
- The microphone foreground listener is NOT started merely because Activity.onResume fired
  during a permission transition.
- If microphone permission is already granted, listening is scheduled after the Activity is ready.
- If the user grants microphone permission, listening is scheduled after the permission callback.
- The wake service refuses to start recognition unless its microphone foreground service was
  successfully promoted to the foreground first.
- Foreground-service startup failures leave Athena open instead of continuing into recognition.
- Existing local AI, UI, PC pairing, accessibility and wake functionality is retained.
