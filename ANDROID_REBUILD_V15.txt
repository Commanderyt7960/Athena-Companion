ATHENA V15 CLEAN ANDROID REBUILD

This rebuild removes the startup background-listener launch that could cause the app to disappear immediately after the Android microphone permission flow.

Startup behaviour:
- MainActivity always stays open regardless of microphone choice.
- Microphone permission is requested from the visible Activity.
- If microphone access is declined, Athena remains open and asks again.
- Notification permission is optional and never closes the app.
- Always-on microphone is an explicit Tools option. It is NOT silently started during app startup.
- Once the user enables Always-on microphone, the foreground microphone service is started while Athena is visible.
- If Android rejects that service, the app stays open and reports the problem instead of crashing.

The Android system controls the wording of its own permission dialog. Athena cannot rename or force Android's choices.
